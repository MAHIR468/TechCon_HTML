Our History

TechCon started in 2010 as a small local gathering of developers and has grown into one of the world’s premier technology conferences. Over the years, it has featured groundbreaking tech trends, major product launches, and global collaboration.

Our Mission

At TechCon, our mission is to connect minds and inspire innovation. We aim to create a platform where technology leaders, developers, students, and businesses come together to share knowledge and shape the future.

Notable Past Speakers

Dr. Ada Lin

AI expert and professor, Dr. Ada Lin is known for her work in ethical machine learning and has led initiatives at several top universities and tech firms.

Marcus Tanev

Founder of GreenCode, Marcus shared his vision on sustainable programming and green tech development at TechCon 2021.

Sophia Ali

Sophia is a cyber-security leader and advocate for women in tech. Her TechCon 2020 keynote inspired thousands of attendees.
